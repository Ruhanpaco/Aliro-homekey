# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Aliro HomeKey to be a project where a bug report, a pin table fix, or
a "this doesn't work on my board" issue gets a useful response regardless of
who sent it — experience level, background, or how the message is worded.
We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Being specific about what you tried, what board you used, and what you saw
  — "should work" isn't a test result here, and it isn't a useful report
  either.
- Being direct about disagreements on technical approach, without making it
  personal.
- Accepting that a beta reader for a physical door lock earns extra scrutiny,
  and that scrutiny isn't hostility.
- Gracefully accepting a "no" or a "not yet" on a contribution, and asking why
  if the reason isn't clear.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in any project space.
- Publishing others' private information — an email, a home network detail
  visible in a log, anything not clearly meant to be public — without
  explicit permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests, or discussion.
- Submitting AI-generated content presented as tested or verified when it
  wasn't — see `CONTRIBUTING.md`'s AI guidelines. This is a conduct issue
  here specifically, because the result runs on hardware that opens a door.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions —
and when someone is representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report a violation to **hi@ruhanpacolli.online**. Include what happened,
where, and any relevant links or screenshots. Reports are handled privately,
and the reporter's identity is not shared beyond what's needed to act on it.

Maintainers who receive a report will:

1. Acknowledge it.
2. Review the context, including the reported person's account of events.
3. Respond with an outcome — anything from no action, to a warning, to a
   temporary or permanent ban from project spaces, scaled to what happened.

Maintainers are held to this standard too. A report about a maintainer's own
conduct goes to the same address and is not exempt from the same process.
