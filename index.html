01 / THE PROJECT
WHY IT EXISTS

Turning a tiny microcontroller into a modern access point.

Commercial smart-access systems hide a remarkable amount of complexity behind a polished tap-to-unlock experience. Aliro HomeKey is an open-source effort to understand and implement the pieces needed to bring that experience to accessible hardware.

At its heart is an ESP32-based prototype. The project investigates the Aliro access-control ecosystem and the protocols and cryptography around digital credentials, making experimentation possible without requiring a proprietary access-control platform.

04 / THE HARD PART
WHY THIS ISN'T JUST AN ARDUINO PROJECT

Most of the interesting work happens where the documentation gets quiet.

Implementing modern digital access is not simply a matter of reading an NFC tag. The project involves protocols, authentication, secure messaging, device behavior and pieces of an ecosystem where documentation can be incomplete, proprietary or difficult to obtain.
