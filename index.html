THE PROJECT

An access reader you can inspect, flash and learn from.

The project turns an ESP32 into an experimental Aliro reader. It combines NFC communication, Aliro credential handling, Matter Door Lock integration, OTA updates and a configuration interface. The goal is not to hide the complexity. It is to make the system understandable.

01 · CREDENTIALS

Digital identity

Explore how a phone credential reaches an embedded reader and becomes an authenticated access transaction.

02 · HARDWARE

Physical interface

ESP32 and PN532 hardware provide a practical platform for testing the reader on real devices.

03 · CONTROL

Lock decision

Matter exposes the access-control device while the firmware connects authentication results to the configured output.
