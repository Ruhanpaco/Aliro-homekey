05 / ROADMAP

Kept honest on purpose.

This page separates what's actually been verified from what's implemented-but-unproven and what's a genuinely open question. The full detail lives in docs/ROADMAP.md — this is the summary.

VERIFIED

Proven on real hardware.

Aliro transaction

Both transaction paths open the lock

Fast path (a previously-seen credential, reusing a stored session key) in ~569 ms; standard path (the full handshake) in ~2047 ms. Both end with the lock GPIO firing.

Matter provisioning

A controller can enroll a credential the standard way

SetAliroReaderConfig gives the lock its reader key pair; SetCredential with an Aliro endpoint key enrolls a phone. Verified with Apple Home, over two commissioned fabrics.

Attestation

Test credentials don't block commissioning

This image carries esp-matter's test attestation credentials, not a real device attestation chain. Apple Home commissions it anyway, after warning the accessory is uncertified. That was expected to be a hard wall — it wasn't.

Product layer

The surrounding pieces work, not just the protocol

Home app lock/unlock, OTA with rollback, the browser configuration UI, MQTT publishing, and Home Assistant discovery have all been exercised on the same board.

IMPLEMENTED, NOT YET RELIABLE

Built, but the evidence says not done.

Apple Wallet Express Mode

The ECP beacon still desyncs the NFC bus

Waking a locked phone needs the reader to emit an Apple ECP frame during polling. Two separate implementation attempts have both been found, on real hardware, to leave the PN532 unable to ACK any further commands after the beacon runs. It's implemented and gated behind a flag that defaults off until the actual desync is root-caused — not just believed fixed a second time.

ESP32-C3 / ESP32-S3

Builds and fits, hasn't been flashed to a board

CI compiles and releases firmware for both, and both pass the OTA-slot-fit check. That's a real signal, but it isn't the same as a tap opening a lock — see Hardware.

OPEN QUESTIONS

Genuinely unknown, not just untested.

Google Wallet and Samsung Wallet

Untested. Samsung specifically needs a Matter-capable hub to administer the fabric during onboarding — a SmartThings Station, a 2022-or-later Samsung TV, a Family Hub fridge, or some soundbars all qualify, but a bare Galaxy phone on its own cannot hold the fabric. Home Assistant's own Matter server can commission the lock with no hub at all, which proves the Matter/Aliro plumbing works under a non-Apple administrator — but it can't put a key in a wallet, so it only answers half the question.

Reader private key storage on a real door

Flash encryption, the DS peripheral, or an external secure element are all options; none is committed to yet.

BLE and UWB

Both are part of the Aliro standard and the reason it's interesting long-term — passive unlock and secure ranging without a tap. esp_aliro_lib is NFC-only today.
